## **5. Engineering mindset — not just technical knowledge**

Ahamed must develop the ability to think like an engineer.

For every assignment, he should learn to follow this sequence:

1. **Understand the problem**

   What is the business problem? Who is affected? What outcome is expected?
2. **Research and analyse**

   What data, technologies, algorithms and approaches are suitable?
3. **Design the solution**

   Define architecture, components, data flows, interfaces and acceptance criteria.
4. **Implement**

   Develop the solution using appropriate engineering practices.
5. **Test and validate**

   Verify functionality, accuracy, edge cases, reliability and business outcomes.
6. **Deploy**

   Make the solution usable in its intended environment.
7. **Monitor and improve**

   Identify failures, performance issues and opportunities for optimisation.
8. **Document and deliver**

   Demonstrate the solution, explain decisions and hand it over professionally.

He must understand not only how to make something work, but also why it works, when it may fail and how to improve it.

## **6. AI-augmented, human-owned engineering**

A fundamental principle of this programme is:

# **AI-Augmented, Human-Owned Engineering**

Ahamed is encouraged to use AI tools as engineering assistants. The objective is not to prevent AI usage. The objective is to ensure that AI accelerates his learning and engineering productivity without replacing his own thinking.

AI may help him:

- Understand unfamiliar concepts.
- Research technologies and compare approaches.
- Explore architecture alternatives.
- Debug errors and explain code.
- Review implementations and identify potential issues.
- Generate test ideas and documentation suggestions.
- Improve development speed and productivity.

However, Ahamed must personally own:

- Problem understanding.
- Technical decisions.
- Architecture choices.
- Code comprehension.
- Validation of AI-generated suggestions.
- Testing and quality assurance.
- Security and responsible data handling.
- Final engineering decisions.
- Delivery accountability.

**The standard is simple: using AI to produce code is not proof of engineering competency. Understanding, validating, explaining, modifying and independently reproducing the solution are the evidence of competency.**

## **7. Independent competency verification**

Every significant assignment must establish four dimensions of capability.

| Stage      | What must be demonstrated                                                                      |
| ---------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Understand | Explain the problem, requirements, assumptions, inputs, outputs and approach.                  |
| Implement  | Build the solution and explain the implementation decisions.                                   |
| Defend     | Explain the code, predict outputs, troubleshoot and modify requirements without relying on AI. |
| Transfer   | Apply the same knowledge to a new but related problem independently.                           |

We will maintain three separate evidence streams:

1. **Engineering evidence:** Code, notebooks, architecture, tests, execution results and deployed applications.
2. **AI interaction evidence:** Prompts, AI responses, decisions, modifications and validation.
3. **Competency evidence:** Explanations, live demonstrations, debugging and independent transfer exercises.

This prevents a completed repository from being mistaken for proven individual capability.
