# Week 1

Week 1 is the restart week. Days 1-4 redo the earlier Day 1 to Day 4 material with new training material from the mentor. Day 5 is the first live check without AI, with an evidence pack and a hand-off to the mentor. The plan has the details in `..\programme\Approved_20_Week_Plan.md`, section 6.

## Where the work goes

Put each day's work in its own folder inside `week-01\`. The folders are created as you start each day. They do not exist yet.

| Folder | Work |
|---|---|
| `day-01\` | Day 1 work |
| `day-02\` | Day 2 work |
| `day-03\` | Day 3 work |
| `day-04\` | Day 4 work |

Day 5 is the live check and the evidence pack. The mentor will say where the Day 5 evidence goes.

Your first-attempt notebooks are in `..\archive\first-attempt-2026-11\`. Leave them as they are. Do the redo in the new day folders.

## What to commit

At the end of every day, commit:

- The code you wrote that day, in that day's folder.
- A short note in your own words on what the code does and what you understood before using any AI help.
- A short record of any AI help you used: what you tried first, what you accepted or changed, and how you tested it.
- Test results or the output that shows the code ran, if the training material asks for it.

Commit small and often, with a plain message such as `day 02: add file reader and checks`.

## Do not commit

- Passwords, keys, tokens or any other secret.
- Real personal, client or customer data. Use the synthetic data the training material gives you.
- Virtual environments, caches and generated output. `.gitignore` already skips these.
